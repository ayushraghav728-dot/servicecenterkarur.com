Common Sony TV Problems Repaired in Dindigul

Frequent issues faced by Sony television owners in Dindigul and how our local technician inspects them.

Blink Code Error Fault #1

Sony TV Red Light Blinking 6 Times

What Happens: Television turns on for two seconds, display remains dark or flashes briefly, then the front light flashes red 6 times, pauses, and repeats.

Why It Happens: The Sony micro-controller detected an open circuit or burned LED diode in the backlight strips and triggered the inverter protection shutdown.

On-Site Diagnostic: Technician uses an LED strip tester to measure each backlight row and verifies the feedback voltage circuit on the power board.
Power Circuit Fault #2

Sony TV Red Light Blinking 2 or 8 Times

What Happens: Front indicator blinks red twice or eight times, the relay clicks repeatedly, and the television refuses to boot into normal operation.

Why It Happens: Secondary voltage rail failure on the Sony G-Board power supply, usually caused by a shorted regulator diode or overloaded capacitor.

On-Site Diagnostic: Technician tests the 12V and 24V supply rails, isolates mainboard draw, and repairs faulty components on the G-Board.
Smart OS Fault #3

Sony TV Stuck on Android Spinning Balls Logo

What Happens: When turned on, the Sony logo appears followed by the colorful spinning Android circles, which continue looping without opening the home menu.

Why It Happens: Corrupted system cache following an interrupted Google TV update, or failing memory sectors on the mainboard eMMC storage.

On-Site Diagnostic: Technician boots into Sony recovery mode, clears the system partition cache, or performs on-site firmware re-installation.
Audio Issue Fault #4

Sony Bravia Speaker Buzzing During Dialogue

What Happens: Sound plays, but voices sound muffled and rattle noticeably during news broadcasts or movies at moderate volume.

Why It Happens: The compact acoustic bass reflex speaker cone edge has split or torn due to heat and age, causing the voice coil to rub against the magnet.

On-Site Diagnostic: Tests speaker resistance with a multimeter and installs a matched replacement Sony acoustic speaker driver pair.
HDMI Port Fault #5

Sony TV HDMI 'No Signal' from Set-Top Box

What Happens: Set-top box is powered on, but all HDMI ports on the Sony TV display 'No Signal' or screen flashes blue repeatedly.

Why It Happens: Loose connector pins from repeated plugging or a damaged HDMI switch IC on the Bravia motherboard following a lightning surge.

On-Site Diagnostic: Tests port pin continuity, inspects the 5V HDMI detection rail, and repairs solder connections or replaces the HDMI switch IC.
Remote Issue Fault #6

Sony Bluetooth Voice Remote Not Pairing

What Happens: Power button turns the TV on via infrared, but directional buttons, microphone voice search, and OK button do not respond.

Why It Happens: Bluetooth pairing lost, mic button firmware desynchronized, or internal Bluetooth transceiver chip on Bravia mainboard has failed.

On-Site Diagnostic: Performs Sony hardware pairing button sequence, clears remote registration in settings, and measures 3.3V Bluetooth antenna rail.

Sony TV Repair Parts & Components

Essential internal components tested and serviced for Sony televisions in Dindigul.

Every television uses distinct circuit boards and display parts. Here is an overview of key Sony TV parts, their function, and the problems they cause when faulty:

Sony SMPS Power Supply Board Power Circuit

Multi-rail regulated power board powering the Bravia XR processor, audio amplifiers, and high-voltage backlight inverter lines.

Fault symptoms: Sony red LED blinking 2 or 8 times, TV clicking without powering on, completely dead standby light.

Sony Main Logic Motherboard Core Logic

Houses the Sony cognitive picture processor, audio DSP, tuner section, and input/output control circuits.

Fault symptoms: Spinning Android circles boot loop, Bravia logo freeze, HDMI ARC no signal, or optical audio failure.

Sony High-Luminance LED Backlight Strips Display Backlight

High-CRI LED diodes mounted behind the Sony Triluminos display panel providing vivid, uniform backlighting.

Fault symptoms: Sony red standby LED blinking 6 times, sound playing clearly with pitch-black screen, flashlight test shows picture.

Sony T-Con Timing Controller Board Display Timing

Converts digital video signals into microsecond pixel timing for the Sony Bravia glass panel.

Fault symptoms: Vertical rainbow stripes across panel, solarized negative colors, or picture freezing while sound plays.

Sony Backlight Boost Regulator Circuit Backlight Regulator

Monitors current balance across LED strings and triggers Bravia 6-blink error code if any diode fails.

Fault symptoms: Backlight blinks for one second upon power-on before TV shuts down into 6-blink red protection mode.

Sony Internal Stereo Acoustic Drivers Audio Driver

Acoustically isolated speaker drivers providing clear dialogue separation and crisp stereo audio.

Fault symptoms: Vibrating buzzing noise during bass frequencies, muffled speech, or one speaker channel completely dead.

Sony HDMI 2.0 / 2.1 & USB Port Connectors Input Interface

High-bandwidth input terminals linking DTH boxes, Blu-ray players, and consoles to Sony Bravia TVs.

Fault symptoms: HDMI port failing to negotiate HDCP handshake, loose connection pin, or 'No Signal' banner.

Sony Wireless Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Transceiver Wireless Module

Internal wireless circuit board providing stable connectivity for Google TV streaming and voice remotes.

Fault symptoms: Bravia showing 'Wi-Fi not connected', failed router scans, or Bluetooth voice search disconnecting.

Sony Standby LED & IR Remote Photodiode Remote Sensor

Captures remote inputs and signals internal fault codes via front-facing multi-color indicator.

Fault symptoms: TV unresponsive to infrared remote, red light blinking without power-on, or sensor eye cracked.

Sony Display Panel High-Speed Ribbon Cables Signal Ribbon

Shielded multi-conductor ribbon transferring high-speed digital video between Bravia boards.

Fault symptoms: Horizontal display noise, ghosting silhouettes on screen, or flickering color bands.
