Common Intex TV Problems Repaired in Karur

Frequent issues faced by Intex television owners in Karur and how our local technician inspects them.

Power Circuit Fault #1

Intex TV Not Turning On / Standby Dead

Customer Symptom: Power cord is plugged in, but the front red standby indicator stays completely dark and the television does not respond.

Likely Component Cause: Mains surge blew the input glass fuse, shorted the bridge rectifier, or damaged the primary switching IC on the combo board.

How We Check & Fix: Tests mains continuity, checks the primary filter capacitor voltage, and replaces shorted diodes and ICs on the combo board.
Backlight Failure Fault #2

Intex Sound Working but Screen is Black

Customer Symptom: Channel sound and dialogue play clearly from the TV speakers, but the picture is missing. Torch test reveals faint video.

Likely Component Cause: Burnt LED diodes in the backlight strips have opened the circuit, causing the driver to shut off screen illumination.

How We Check & Fix: Measures forward voltage across each backlight strip with an LED tester and installs a brand-matched backlight array.
Audio Issue Fault #3

Harsh Rattling Sound from Intex Speakers

Customer Symptom: Voices sound heavily distorted and vibrate unpleasantly inside the TV cabinet when volume is raised above 20.

Likely Component Cause: Torn paper cone surround on the downward-firing speakers due to heat and prolonged high volume playback.

How We Check & Fix: Inspects speaker cone paper integrity, tests 8-ohm voice coil impedance, and fits a fresh stereo speaker pair.
Smart OS Fault #4

Intex Smart TV Stuck on Opening Logo

Customer Symptom: The television powers on, displays the 'Intex' or 'Smart' logo, and remains frozen on that screen indefinitely.

Likely Component Cause: Corrupted Android system files, failed app update, or read/write errors on the motherboard eMMC flash chip.

How We Check & Fix: Enters system recovery mode, clears cached partition data, and reflashes stable system firmware onto the board.
Signal Input Fault #5

Intex HDMI Input Shows 'No Signal'

Customer Symptom: Set-top box is powered on and cable is connected, but Intex screen displays 'No Signal' banner across HDMI ports.

Likely Component Cause: Broken or loose pins inside the HDMI socket or damaged 5V detection diodes from lightning induced line surges.

How We Check & Fix: Tests pin continuity with a multimeter, resolders loose board tracks, or replaces the damaged physical HDMI port.
Remote Receiver Fault #6

Intex TV Unresponsive to Remote Handset

Customer Symptom: Remote handset is working with fresh batteries, but the television does not change channels or respond to the power button.

Likely Component Cause: Faulty infrared photodiode on the front sensor PCB or broken ribbon cable connecting the sensor to the mainboard.

How We Check & Fix: Tests 3.3V standby supply rail to the IR receiver and installs a new photodiode sensor eye if defective.

Standard HDMI and USB sockets accommodating set-top boxes on Intex televisions.

Fault symptoms: Intex HDMI socket loose and flickering when touched, or pendrive not detected.

Intex Wireless Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Transceiver Wireless Module

Compact Wi-Fi receiver module enabling internet access for Intex smart TV features.

Fault symptoms: Intex TV unable to connect to home Wi-Fi, saved network forgotten, or slow app loading.

Intex Standby LED & IR Remote Photodiode Remote Sensor

Universal infrared photodiode receiver mounted on front bezel of Intex television.

Fault symptoms: Intex TV not responding to remote control, red standby light dead, or delayed channel change.
