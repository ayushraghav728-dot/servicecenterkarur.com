Common Panasonic TV Problems Repaired in Karur

Frequent issues faced by Panasonic television owners in Karur and how our local technician inspects them.

Backlight Failure Fault #1

Audio Plays Clearly but Panasonic Screen is Black

Customer Symptom: Serial dialogue and news broadcasts are audible, but the television picture has gone totally black. A torch light held against the panel shows faint picture silhouettes.

Likely Component Fault: Series LED diodes inside the Viera backlight array have burnt open, interrupting the voltage loop and triggering driver shutdown.

How We Check & Fix: Technician opens the rear chassis carefully, tests each strip using an LED tester, and installs a fresh matched replacement backlight set.
Power Circuit Fault #2

Panasonic TV Completely Dead / No Power Light

Customer Symptom: AC cord is firmly connected, but the front indicator LED stays dead and television does not respond to remote or cabinet keys.

Likely Component Fault: High voltage surge destroyed the input fuse, damaged the bridge rectifier, or shorted switching MOSFETs on the TNPA power supply board.

How We Check & Fix: Technician checks fuse continuity, tests the 300V primary filter capacitor, and replaces damaged semiconductors at board level.
Smart OS Fault #3

Panasonic TV Stuck on Viera Startup Logo

Customer Symptom: TV switches on, displays the Viera emblem, freezes for several minutes, and reboots in a repetitive loop without loading channels.

Likely Component Fault: Firmware partition corruption on the eMMC flash storage chip or unstable 1.2V core voltage rails on the logic motherboard.

How We Check & Fix: Technician connects via USB service mode to clear cache memory, tests motherboard voltage rails, or reflashes manufacturer firmware.
Display Timing Fault #4

Double Ghosting Image & Solarized Colors on Panasonic

Customer Symptom: Actors appear with double outlines or colors look like a photo negative with severe wash-out across the screen.

Likely Component Fault: Gamma reference voltage IC failure on the T-Con timing controller board or degraded LVDS cable pins connecting panel to motherboard.

How We Check & Fix: Measures 14-point gamma reference voltages on the T-Con board and cleans oxidized LVDS ribbon cable contacts.
Audio Issue Fault #5

Panasonic TV Sound Cuts Out or Vibrates Heavily

Customer Symptom: Sound plays normally for a few minutes then cuts out, or internal speakers buzz loudly whenever bass or background music plays.

Likely Component Fault: Thermal protection shutdown in the audio power amplifier IC or cracked foam cone surround on the internal speaker drivers.

How We Check & Fix: Tests audio IC operating temperature and replaces cracked speaker units with matched Panasonic acoustic drivers.
Remote Sensor Fault #6

Panasonic TV IR Eye Not Detecting Remote Commands

Customer Symptom: Remote control works on camera test, but the television front indicator LED fails to blink or respond when buttons are pressed.

Likely Component Fault: Defective infrared photodiode receiver on the front sensor board or lost 3.3V standby supply rail from the mainboard.

How We Check & Fix: Tests the infrared photodiode signal line and checks 3.3V standby voltage with a multimeter, replacing the front sensor eye if burned.

Panasonic TV Repair Parts & Components

Essential internal components tested and serviced for Panasonic televisions in Karur.

Every television uses distinct circuit boards and display parts. Here is an overview of key Panasonic TV parts, their function, and the problems they cause when faulty:

Panasonic SMPS Power Supply Board Power Circuit

Japanese-engineered power module feeding stabilized current to the Hexa Chroma processor and IPS panel backlights.

Fault symptoms: Front power indicator refusing to switch on, power cycling on startup, or blown fuse after power fluctuations.

Panasonic Main Logic Motherboard Core Logic

Controls wide-gamut 6-color reproduction, digital TV reception, and smart streaming operations.

Fault symptoms: Stuck on Viera startup screen, unexpected restarts during movie playback, or unresponsive control buttons.

Panasonic High-Luminance LED Backlight Strips Display Backlight

High-efficiency LED diode strips designed to illuminate wide-viewing-angle IPS display panels evenly.

Fault symptoms: Audio heard clearly but screen pitch dark, dim shadow at panel edges, or flickering picture brightness.

Panasonic T-Con Timing Controller Board Display Timing

Routes pixel timing clock lines and gamma voltages to the Panasonic IPS display matrix.

Fault symptoms: Negative color inversion, white display with sound, or fine horizontal scanning lines.

Panasonic Backlight Boost Regulator Circuit Backlight Regulator

Regulates constant current across multiple backlight rows with built-in over-voltage protection.

Fault symptoms: Backlight illuminates for two seconds then extinguishes, or intermittent dimming during dark movie scenes.

Panasonic Internal Stereo Acoustic Drivers Audio Driver

Acoustic driver units engineered to project clean vocals and background audio into family living rooms.

Fault symptoms: Buzzing sound on loud serials, low volume even at maximum setting, or distorted dialogue.

Panasonic HDMI 2.0 / 2.1 & USB Port Connectors Input Interface

Gold-plated inputs accommodating set-top box cables, DVD players, and audio gear on Panasonic TVs.

Fault symptoms: Set-top box video cutting out intermittently, bent connector pins, or loose HDMI fit.

Panasonic Wireless Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Transceiver Wireless Module

Built-in Wi-Fi adapter facilitating YouTube streaming and screen mirror functions on Viera TVs.

Fault symptoms: Continuous network disconnection, failure to discover home Wi-Fi SSID, or slow video buffering.

Panasonic Standby LED & IR Remote Photodiode Remote Sensor

Front-facing infrared photodiode receiving remote controller signals across household distances.

Fault symptoms: Viera TV not responding to remote handset, standby indicator failing to turn green, or receiver dead.

Panasonic Display Panel High-Speed Ribbon Cables Signal Ribbon

Ultra-thin multi-track flex cable connecting main signal outputs to the panel driver circuits.

Fault symptoms: Intermittent vertical line appearing when bezel is pressed, color jitter, or partial image blanking.
