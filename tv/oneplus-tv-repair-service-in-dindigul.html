Common OnePlus TV Problems Repaired in Dindigul

Frequent issues faced by OnePlus television owners in Dindigul and how our local technician inspects them.

Smart OS Fault #1

OnePlus TV Stuck on Spinning Dots Screen

Customer Symptom: When switched on, the television shows the OnePlus logo followed by four spinning colored dots that loop indefinitely.

Likely Component Fault: Corrupted OxygenPlay system partition, uncompleted automatic software update, or eMMC storage read/write error.

How We Check & Fix: Accesses Android recovery console, wipes cache partition, or reflashes official OnePlus firmware on-site.
Backlight Failure Fault #2

Audio Plays Normally but OnePlus Screen is Dark

Customer Symptom: Dialogue and channel audio play loud and clear, but the display remains pitch black. Faint video shows under mobile torch.

Likely Component Fault: One or more LED diodes in the backlight strips have burned open, breaking the circuit and shutting off illumination.

How We Check & Fix: Measures forward voltage across each backlight strip with an LED tester and installs model-matched replacement strips.
Display Timing Fault #3

Bright Green Vertical Line on OnePlus Display

Customer Symptom: A sharp green, white, or pink vertical line runs straight down the screen from top to bottom during all video playback.

Likely Component Fault: Thermal stress or moisture affecting panel gate driver (COF) bonding, or loose LVDS flat ribbon cable seating.

How We Check & Fix: Inspects source PCB connections, tests T-Con mini-LVDS data lines, and reseats ribbon cables with anti-static care.
Remote Control Fault #4

OnePlus Bluetooth Voice Remote Unpairing

Customer Symptom: Remote handset stops controlling the TV, and holding buttons fails to reconnect with the television Bluetooth receiver.

Likely Component Fault: Desynchronized Bluetooth pairing cache, depleted battery current, or failing Bluetooth transceiver module on mainboard.

How We Check & Fix: Performs hardware pairing reset, verifies 3.3V power supply to the wireless card, and updates remote firmware.
Power Circuit Fault #5

OnePlus TV Dead Standby Light After Voltage Spike

Customer Symptom: Power cord is connected to mains, but the small white/red standby indicator at center bezel is completely dark.

Likely Component Fault: A mains surge ruptured the input fuse, shorted primary switching MOSFETs, or damaged the SMPS controller IC.

How We Check & Fix: Tests AC input fuse, checks primary filter capacitor charge, and replaces shorted semiconductor parts on the power unit.
Audio Issue Fault #6

Dolby Audio Speaker Rattle During Dialogue

Customer Symptom: Sound plays, but voices sound raspy and produce an annoying cabinet rattle, especially on bass frequencies.

Likely Component Fault: Internal speaker cone surround has torn due to prolonged vibration or loose acoustic driver mounting screws.

How We Check & Fix: Inspects speaker cone paper integrity, measures voice coil impedance, and installs a fresh stereo sound driver pair.
