Common Havells Washing Machine Problems We Check in Dindigul

If your Havells washing machine shows any of these common symptoms, our technicians inspect the components to identify the exact cause:

Havells Spin Motor Not Working / Humming

The spin tub does not rotate when the timer is turned on, only a faint buzzing sound is heard. Caused by a jammed motor bushing, cut brake wire, or weak capacitor.

Havells Wash Pulsator Turning Weakly

The motor runs but the wash plate cannot turn clothes properly. Caused by a worn or loose V-belt, stripped pulsator splines, or worn gearbox.

Havells Water Leaking From Bottom

Water drips continuously under the machine onto the floor. Caused by a cracked drain pipe, loose hose clamp, or torn spin shaft buffer seal.

Havells Wash Timer Knob Slipping / Stuck

The wash timer does not advance or clicks without moving the pulsator. The mechanical timer contacts are burnt or the spring gears are broken.

Havells Heavy Vibration During Spin

The spin tub bangs wildly against the outer cabinet. Caused by unbalance, loose motor mounting springs, or broken top load suspension rods.

Havells Water Continuously Draining Out

Water filled into the wash tub drains out immediately. Caused by coins, buttons, or lint wedged in the rubber drain valve seat.
