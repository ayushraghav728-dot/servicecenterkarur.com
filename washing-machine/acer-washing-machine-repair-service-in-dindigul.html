Common Acer Washing Machine Problems We Check in Dindigul

If your Acer washing machine shows any of these common symptoms, our technicians inspect the components to identify the exact cause:

Acer E1 Water Inlet Error

Machine beeps and halts because water does not reach the wash level in time. Caused by clogged inlet filter screens or a burnt solenoid coil.

Acer E2 Drain Problem

Dirty water remains inside the drum after washing. Caused by lint, coins, or pins jammed in the drain pump filter or a burnt drain pump motor.

Acer E3 Unbalanced Spin Error

The machine stops before spinning and shakes heavily. Caused by tangled clothes or worn-out suspension damper rods.

Acer Door Lock Stuck / Latch Issue

Front load door will not open after cycle completes or machine does not start due to open door signal. The bi-metal interlock switch needs replacement.

Acer Drum Not Turning / Motor Silent

The timer advances but the drum or pulsator will not move. Caused by a snapped drive belt, worn motor capacitor, or inverter board issue.

Acer Water Continuously Draining Out

Water flows directly out of the drain pipe as soon as the tap is turned on. Caused by foreign particles wedged under the drain valve rubber seal.
