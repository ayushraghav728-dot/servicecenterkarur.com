Liebherr Refrigerator Parts We Check or Replace

Simple explanations of essential components tested during our doorstep service visits in Karur.

DuoCooling Dual Evaporator Fan

Liebherr fridges use two separate cooling circuits for the freezer and fridge. If the fridge fan fails, the freezer works fine while the main cabin loses chill.

BioFresh Precision Temperature Sensor

Monitors the near-0°C BioFresh drawer for fresh meat and produce. If the sensor goes out of calibration, food inside can freeze or lose freshness.

Variable Speed Inverter Compressor

Pumps refrigerant according to cooling demand. If it fails to start or buzzes quietly without compressing, the inverter driver and windings are tested.

Defrost Heating Element

Melts ice off the evaporator coils automatically. When the element breaks, ice accumulates until all airflow vents are completely blocked.

Electronic Main Logic Board

Controls dual-zone temperatures, door open alarms, and compressor speed. Power fluctuations can corrupt or burn out components on this board.

Magnetic Cabinet Door Gasket

A heavy-duty rubber seal that prevents outside heat and humidity from entering. If misaligned or cracked, moisture droplets form on shelves.

Internal Air Circulation Duct

Directs chilled air evenly behind shelves. If obstructed by ice, the technician clears the blockage and tests the defrost system.

R600a Refrigeration Sealed System

Transfers heat out of the fridge using eco-friendly refrigerant. If a leak occurs, both compartments gradually stop cooling.

When Should You Call for Liebherr Refrigerator Repair?

Look out for these warning signs to avoid food spoilage and costly compressor replacements.

  • ❄️ Cooling becoming low: Fresh food or milk spoiling quickly on lower shelves.
  • 🧊 Freezer not freezing: Ice cubes not setting or ice cream melting inside the freezer.
  • 💧 Water leakage: Water pooling beneath the vegetable crisper or leaking on the kitchen floor.
  • 🧱 Excess frost buildup: Dense ice accumulating on the freezer back wall, choking air vents.
  • 🔊 Compressor noise or clicking: Repeated clicking sounds every 2 to 3 minutes without cooling.
  • 🚪 Door not sealing: Moisture droplets forming around door frames due to weak magnetic gaskets.
  • ⚠️ Repeated tripping or blinking: Fridge cutting power or digital display flashing error codes.
  • ⚡ Unusual electrical smell: If you smell burning insulation, switch off the appliance immediately, unplug the cable, and call for inspection.
