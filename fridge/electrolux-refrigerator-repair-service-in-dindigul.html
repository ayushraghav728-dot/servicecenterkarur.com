Airflow Issue

Fresh Food Section Losing Chill

Freezer holds sub-zero cold, but items in the upper or lower fresh food section remain warm.

Probable Cause: Defrost sensor or multi-flow fan motor failure
Technician Check: Inspects evaporator coil ice buildup and fan rotation

Starter Circuit

Clicking Relay Sound Behind Refrigerator

A repeated clicking sound occurs every few minutes with zero cooling production.

Probable Cause: PTC starter relay or overload protector failure
Technician Check: Measures relay resistance and compressor winding health
Resolution: Installs new matched starter relay assembly
Drainage Issue

Water Gathering in TasteLock Crisper

Excess condensation collects beneath vegetable drawers, spoiling leafy greens.

Probable Cause: Clogged defrost drain tube or blocked drain trough
Technician Check: Inspects drain channel and flushes debris
Resolution: Cleans and flushes defrost drainage pathway
Fan Assembly

Vibrating Fan Noise from Freezer Duct

A rattling or whirring noise occurs inside the freezer and stops when the door is opened.

Probable Cause: Fan blade contacting ice frost or worn motor bearing
Technician Check: Checks blade clearance and motor shaft play
Resolution: Clears coil frost and lubricates or replaces fan motor
Control Board

NutriFresh Inverter PCB Unresponsive

The refrigerator has power at the wall outlet but will not illuminate or turn on the motor.

Probable Cause: Power surge damage to main control motherboard
Technician Check: Tests input fuse, varistor, and DC regulator stages
Resolution: Repairs power circuitry or replaces control board
Refrigerant Leak

Continuous Compressor Run with Zero Ice

The motor runs warm continuously, but the freezer is unable to freeze water.

Probable Cause: Refrigerant leak in sealed aluminum or copper tubing
Technician Check: Performs pressure leak test on sealed circuit
Resolution: Brazes leak joint, vacuums lines, and recharges R600a

Electrolux Refrigerator Parts We Check or Replace

Simple explanations of essential components tested during our doorstep service visits in Dindigul.

NutriFresh Inverter Compressor

Runs at varying speeds to save power while keeping temperatures steady. If it does not start or makes strange rattling sounds, the technician inspects it.

TasteLock Humidity Control Membrane

Maintains optimal humidity levels in the vegetable crisper. If damaged or misplaced, vegetables lose moisture quickly or collect standing water.

Multi-Flow Evaporator Fan Motor

Pushes chilled air through multiple back-panel vents. If the fan bearing wears out, it makes a grinding sound or fails to push cold air to lower shelves.

Defrost Sensor & Thermal Fuse Assembly

Monitors the cooling coil temperature and turns on the heater to clear frost. A failed sensor causes ice to choke the cooling duct.

Electronic Control PCB

The brain of the refrigerator that coordinates inverter speeds, defrost timings, and door alarms. Voltage spikes can trip or damage this board.

Magnetic Door Gasket

Creates a tight seal along the cabinet perimeter. When the rubber hardens or tears, warm air gets in and creates heavy frost.

Defrost Heater Element

Heats the cooling coil periodically to melt accumulated frost. If open-circuit, cooling drops drastically within two to three days.

Defrost Drain Tube

Takes melted frost water to the rear compressor tray. If blocked, water leaks out into the bottom drawers of the fridge.

When Should You Call for Electrolux Refrigerator Repair?

Look out for these warning signs to avoid food spoilage and costly compressor replacements.

  • ❄️ Cooling becoming low: Fresh food or milk spoiling quickly on lower shelves.
  • 🧊 Freezer not freezing: Ice cubes not setting or ice cream melting inside the freezer.
  • 💧 Water leakage: Water pooling beneath the vegetable crisper or leaking on the kitchen floor.
  • 🧱 Excess frost buildup: Dense ice accumulating on the freezer back wall, choking air vents.
  • 🔊 Compressor noise or clicking: Repeated clicking sounds every 2 to 3 minutes without cooling.
  • 🚪 Door not sealing: Moisture droplets forming around door frames due to weak magnetic gaskets.
  • ⚠️ Repeated tripping or blinking: Fridge cutting power or digital display flashing error codes.
  • ⚡ Unusual electrical smell: If you smell burning insulation, switch off the appliance immediately, unplug the cable, and call for inspection.
