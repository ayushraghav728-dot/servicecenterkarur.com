Samsung Refrigerator Parts We Check or Replace

Simple explanations of essential components tested during our doorstep service visits in Karur.

Digital Inverter Compressor

The inverter compressor pumps cooling gas at varying speeds. If the fridge is not cooling, making repeated clicking noises, or not turning on at all, the compressor windings and driver must be tested.

Twin Cooling Evaporator Fan Motor

This fan blows cold air through the freezer and fridge air channels. When the fan stops or makes buzzing sounds, the freezer may stay cold but the bottom food cabin becomes warm.

Defrost Sensor & Thermal Fuse

The defrost sensor checks how much ice has formed on the cooling coil. If it goes bad, the coil gets completely choked with ice, stopping airflow into the fridge.

Defrost Heater Element

The heater turns on at set intervals to melt excess frost from the cooling coils. When it burns out, thick ice builds up behind the back panel and cooling drops.

Main Inverter PCB Control Board

The electronic PCB manages temperature sensors, fan speed, and inverter compressor frequency. Power surges can damage this board, causing error lights or complete power loss.

Convertible Mode Air Damper

In convertible Samsung models, this motorised flap opens and closes to redirect air when switching between fridge and freezer modes. If stuck, temperature control fails.

Magnetic Door Gasket

The rubber door seal holds cold air inside and keeps warm Karur air out. If the gasket is torn, loose, or hardened, moisture gathers inside and cooling leaks out.

Drain Hole & Evaporation Tray

Melted water from the daily defrost cycle flows through a drain pipe into a tray over the compressor. If dust or algae chokes this pipe, water leaks under the vegetable box.

When Should You Call for Samsung Refrigerator Repair?

Look out for these warning signs to avoid food spoilage and costly compressor replacements.

  • ❄️ Cooling becoming low: Fresh food or milk spoiling quickly on lower shelves.
  • 🧊 Freezer not freezing: Ice cubes not setting or ice cream melting inside the freezer.
  • 💧 Water leakage: Water pooling beneath the vegetable crisper or leaking on the kitchen floor.
  • 🧱 Excess frost buildup: Dense ice accumulating on the freezer back wall, choking air vents.
  • 🔊 Compressor noise or clicking: Repeated clicking sounds every 2 to 3 minutes without cooling.
  • 🚪 Door not sealing: Moisture droplets forming around door frames due to weak magnetic gaskets.
  • ⚠️ Repeated tripping or blinking: Fridge cutting power or digital display flashing error codes.
  • ⚡ Unusual electrical smell: If you smell burning insulation, switch off the appliance immediately, unplug the cable, and call for inspection.
