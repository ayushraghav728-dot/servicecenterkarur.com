Thermostat Failure

Single Door Freezer Box Over-Freezing

Ice builds into a dense solid rock inside the freezer box, making it impossible to close the flap.

Probable Cause: Thermostat contact welded or sensing capillary dislodged
Technician Check: Tests cut-off temperature with a multimeter and ice water

Starter Relay

Compressor Clicking Sound Every Few Minutes

A clicking sound is heard from behind the fridge every 2 to 3 minutes, but the cooling pump never starts.

Probable Cause: Burnt PTC starter relay or open overload protector
Technician Check: Tests relay resistance and compressor winding health
Resolution: Replaces starter relay and overload protector
Defrost Failure

Eon Double Door Lower Cabin Warm

Freezer freezes ice cubes properly, but the lower compartment has no cooling and milk curdles.

Probable Cause: Defrost timer or bimetal failure causing iced evaporator
Technician Check: Tests defrost timer motor and measures heater resistance
Resolution: Replaces defrost timer and clears ice blockage
Drain Line Clogged

Water Leaking Beneath Vegetable Drawer

Defrost water overflows into the vegetable tray rather than draining into the rear compressor pan.

Probable Cause: Defrost drain hole choked with dust or food particles
Technician Check: Inspects drain trough and rear outlet hose
Resolution: Cleans and flushes drainage line with warm water
Door Gasket

Door Gasket Loose with Cold Air Escape

Cold air escapes along the door frame, causing high electricity bills and moisture buildup.

Probable Cause: Rubber gasket hardened, cracked, or lost magnetic grip
Technician Check: Inspects seal contact around the entire perimeter
Resolution: Replaces magnetic rubber door gasket
Refrigerant Leak

Compressor Running Continuously Without Chill

The compressor runs warm continuously, but the interior shelves remain completely room temperature.

Probable Cause: Pinhole gas leak in copper tubing or capillary choke
Technician Check: Checks suction pressure and inspects brazed joints
Resolution: Brazes leak spot, pulls vacuum, and recharges gas

Godrej Refrigerator Parts We Check or Replace

Simple explanations of essential components tested during our doorstep service visits in Dindigul.

Reciprocating / Inverter Compressor

The compressor pumps cooling gas through the condenser and evaporator. If it trips the home fuse or makes clicking noises without cooling, it needs checking.

PTC Starter Relay & Overload Protector

Starts the compressor motor safely and cuts power during voltage fluctuations. Clicking sounds usually indicate a burnt starter relay.

Direct Cool Mechanical Thermostat

Found in Edge single door models to set cooling levels. When it fails, vegetables in the bottom tray freeze into ice or the fridge does not cool at all.

Frost-Free Evaporator Fan Motor

Circulates cold air from the freezer coil into the food compartment in Eon double door models. If stuck or noisy, lower cabin cooling stops.

Defrost Timer & Bimetal Switch

Controls the automatic defrost cycle in frost-free models. If the timer stops turning, heavy frost blocks the cooling vents.

Defrost Heater Element

Melts frost off the cooling coil so air can pass through. If the heater is open, thick ice forms behind the plastic freezer panel.

Magnetic Door Gasket

Ensures the door shuts tight against the cabinet. If loose or cracked, cold air leaks out and electricity consumption goes up.

Drain Pipe & Condensation Tray

Drains melted defrost water outside. If clogged with dust, water collects inside the vegetable tray.

When Should You Call for Godrej Refrigerator Repair?

Look out for these warning signs to avoid food spoilage and costly compressor replacements.

  • ❄️ Cooling becoming low: Fresh food or milk spoiling quickly on lower shelves.
  • 🧊 Freezer not freezing: Ice cubes not setting or ice cream melting inside the freezer.
  • 💧 Water leakage: Water pooling beneath the vegetable crisper or leaking on the kitchen floor.
  • 🧱 Excess frost buildup: Dense ice accumulating on the freezer back wall, choking air vents.
  • 🔊 Compressor noise or clicking: Repeated clicking sounds every 2 to 3 minutes without cooling.
  • 🚪 Door not sealing: Moisture droplets forming around door frames due to weak magnetic gaskets.
  • ⚠️ Repeated tripping or blinking: Fridge cutting power or digital display flashing error codes.
  • ⚡ Unusual electrical smell: If you smell burning insulation, switch off the appliance immediately, unplug the cable, and call for inspection.
