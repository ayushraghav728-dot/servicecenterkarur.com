Hitachi Refrigerator Parts We Check or Replace

Simple explanations of essential components tested during our doorstep service visits in Dindigul.

Dual Fan Cooling Dedicated Motors

Hitachi fridges use separate fans for the freezer and refrigerator compartments. If one fan stops, cooling drops in that specific section.

Inverter Compressor Driver Board

Regulates compressor motor speed based on cooling requirements. If the board fails, the compressor will not turn on.

Eco Thermo-Sensor Assembly

Detects micro-temperature changes on each shelf. If damaged, cooling becomes erratic or the compressor runs without stopping.

Defrost Heater Element

Heats up during automatic defrost to clear the evaporator coil. When the heater burns out, ice blocks the air channels.

Magnetic Door Gasket

A heavy-duty rubber seal preventing cold air leakage on large French door models. If loose, moisture forms on the door dividers.

Main Logic Control Board

The brain of the refrigerator managing dual fans, inverter drive, and touch panel. Power fluctuations can damage this board.

Defrost Drain Channel

Directs melted frost water to the evaporation tray. When clogged, water overflows inside the bottom storage bin.

Sealed R600a Refrigeration Circuit

Carries refrigerant gas through cooling tubes. If a joint leaks gas, the compressor runs warm without producing any cooling.

When Should You Call for Hitachi Refrigerator Repair?

Look out for these warning signs to avoid food spoilage and costly compressor replacements.

  • ❄️ Cooling becoming low: Fresh food or milk spoiling quickly on lower shelves.
  • 🧊 Freezer not freezing: Ice cubes not setting or ice cream melting inside the freezer.
  • 💧 Water leakage: Water pooling beneath the vegetable crisper or leaking on the kitchen floor.
  • 🧱 Excess frost buildup: Dense ice accumulating on the freezer back wall, choking air vents.
  • 🔊 Compressor noise or clicking: Repeated clicking sounds every 2 to 3 minutes without cooling.
  • 🚪 Door not sealing: Moisture droplets forming around door frames due to weak magnetic gaskets.
  • ⚠️ Repeated tripping or blinking: Fridge cutting power or digital display flashing error codes.
  • ⚡ Unusual electrical smell: If you smell burning insulation, switch off the appliance immediately, unplug the cable, and call for inspection.
